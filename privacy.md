Privacy Policy — Home Assistant Banking


Last updated: 8 November 2026


Purpose


This application is used exclusively for personal financial monitoring through a self-hosted Home Assistant installation. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to third parties.


Data Access


With the account holder's explicit consent, the application accesses banking account information through Enable Banking, including account balances and, where authorized, transaction information.


Data Storage and Security


Banking information is processed within the account holder's private Home Assistant environment. Application credentials are stored locally and access is restricted.


No banking information is intentionally published or sold to third parties.


Third-Party Services


Enable Banking and the connected financial institutions process information according to their respective privacy policies.


Data Retention and Access


The account holder controls the Home Assistant installation and may disconnect banking access and delete locally stored information.


Contact


For privacy-related questions, contact the application administrator through the contact email registered with Enable Banking.
